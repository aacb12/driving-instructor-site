Frequently asked questions

How many lessons will I need?

It varies from person to person. Most learners need somewhere between 30 and 45 hours of lessons. After your first lesson I'll give you an honest estimate.

Do I need a provisional licence?

Yes, you'll need a valid provisional licence before your first lesson. Bring it with you so I can check it.

Can you pick me up from school or work?

Yes. I can pick you up and drop you off anywhere within my local area.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice. Lessons cancelled with less notice may be charged in full.
